ACMCA

About the Advanced Certificate Diploma in Multilingual Computer Application (ACMCA)
The **Advanced Certificate Diploma in Multilingual Computer Application (ACMCA)** is a computer education program designed to develop practical computer skills and knowledge of multilingual computing. The course helps students learn computer applications, office productivity tools, internet usage, and working with multiple languages on digital platforms.
**Course Duration:** [1 YEAR]
**Eligibility:** [10th,+2]
**Course Highlights:**
- Computer Fundamentals and Operating Systems
- MS Office and Office Productivity Tools
- Internet and Email Applications
- Data Entry and Typing Skills
- Multilingual Computing and Language Tools
- Practical Computer Applications

**Career Opportunities:** After completing the course, students can explore opportunities in data entry, computer operations, office administration, documentation, and multilingual typing. **Enhance your digital skills and prepare for new career opportunities with the ACMCA course.**

About our institute

Skills for a brighter future

SIVT Bangana (Skill Development Institute) was established in November 2022 with a vision to provide quality computer education and skill development. Our institute focuses on practical learning and career-oriented training. We offer various computer courses to enhance students' technical knowledge and digital skills. Our programs include O Level, ACMCA, Web Designing, Python Programming, AI, and Cyber Security. We aim to prepare students for employment and future career opportunities. We encourage innovation, creativity, and continuous learning in the digital era. **SIVT Bangana – Learn Today, Lead Tomorrow!**
